## App title

## Short description
Create a free trial website and track your C-Net website projects securely.

## Full description
C-Net Web Services helps individuals, institutions and businesses establish a professional digital presence.

Use the app to explore domain, hosting, website development, SEO and digital promotion services. Create a free trial website, sign in securely through email OTP, open your trial website and monitor the progress of final website projects from one convenient place.

Key features:
- Create and open a free trial website
- Secure client login with registered email OTP
- View trial website status and expiry
- Track final website project and deployment progress
- Explore domain, hosting, web design, SEO and promotion services
- Contact C-Net Web Services through call, email or WhatsApp
- Dedicated Admin / Staff access for authorised team members
- Privacy Policy and account/data deletion controls

C-Net Web Services is operated from MCI Campus, Quamruddin Ganj, Bihar Sharif, Nalanda, Bihar, India.

## Data safety working declaration
- Personal information: name, email address, phone number and business details are collected when users submit trial/enquiry forms.
- Authentication: email OTP and secure access-token information are processed for client login.
- App activity: trial and project records are displayed to the authenticated client.
- Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.
- Data is not sold and is not used for third-party behavioural advertising.
- Users may request account and associated-data deletion in the app or through the public deletion URL.

The Play Console Data safety form must match the production app and every enabled third-party service at the time of submission.
